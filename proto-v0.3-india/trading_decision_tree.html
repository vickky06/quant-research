Quick reference:
Mean Reversion Buy → RSI < 30, price below 20-day MA, support confirmed by wicks, volume drying up. Hold 5–15 days.
Momentum Buy → Uptrend on high volume, RSI 30–60. Hold 20–40 days.
Exit rule (MR) → RSI reaches 50 OR price hits resistance ceiling.
Exit rule (Momentum) → RSI hits 70 OR price breaks a higher low on high volume.
Stop loss → Always place below the nearest confirmed support floor.
